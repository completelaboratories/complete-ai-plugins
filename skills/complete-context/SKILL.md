---
name: complete-context
description: Plan and coordinate workouts and activities with other people in Complete when the user asks to set one up, check one, or invite people.
---

Use list-complete-activities and get-complete-activity to find the user's activities, and get-complete-activity-room to see who has joined. Use propose-complete-activity and edit-complete-activity to draft or change a private activity, and plan-complete-activity to choose a workout from Complete's catalog. Before planning, read the user's saved preferences with complete-activity-preferences so plans respect hard limits; save, change or remove a preference only when the user asks. After plan-complete-activity, use complete-activity-plan-choice to save the option the user picks or an edit they ask for. Use complete-activity-groups to match a name the user says to someone they have done an activity with or to a saved group, and to save or remove a group when they ask; it reports ambiguity instead of guessing, adds no strangers and looks nobody up.

Invitations, RSVPs, time offers and accepting an exact time are actions the user takes in the Complete card; never claim they happened unless Complete reports it. Joining is not accepting a time, and a confirmed time is not a delivered calendar invitation until Complete says so. Complete cannot read health records or other people's calendars, and does not accept health conditions as preferences. Which tools appear depends on what is enabled for the user's account.
