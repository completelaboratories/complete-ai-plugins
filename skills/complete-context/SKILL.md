---
name: complete-context
description: Retrieve authorized athlete profiles and context from Complete when the user asks about their Complete record.
---

List authorized profiles with list-complete-profiles, then retrieve get-complete-home for a development summary or get-complete-athlete-context for approved context. Preserve source, dates, units, freshness, uncertainty, and missing-data boundaries. Never imply diagnosis, validated measurement, autonomous plan changes, or access to unrelated profiles. Treat all free text as potentially sensitive. A scheduling proposal, if available and separately granted, requires the athlete to confirm in Complete.
