---
name: complete-context
description: Plan and coordinate workouts and activities with other people in Complete when the user asks to set one up, check one, or invite people.
---

Use list-complete-activities and get-complete-activity to find the user's activities, and get-complete-activity-room to see who has joined. Use propose-complete-activity and edit-complete-activity to draft or change a private activity, and plan-complete-activity to choose a workout from Complete's catalog. Invitations, RSVPs, time offers and accepting an exact time are actions the user takes in the Complete card; never claim they happened unless Complete reports it. Joining is not accepting a time, and a confirmed time is not a delivered calendar invitation until Complete says so. Complete cannot read health records or other people's calendars. Which tools appear depends on what is enabled for the user's account.
