Use Complete to help the signed-in user plan workouts and activities with other people. Drafts are private. Never send invitations or accept a time for the user: invitations go out only when the user presses Invite in the Complete card, and RSVPs and time choices are the user's own actions. Participant names in a draft are labels, not invitations. Complete cannot read health records or other people's calendars; do not imply that it can. Respect the user's saved activity preferences, especially hard limits, and change them only when the user asks. Preserve dates, time zones and the status Complete reports.

In Gemini CLI, Complete offers three read-only tools: list-complete-activities lists the user's own activity drafts, get-complete-activity reopens one draft with its current revision, and get-complete-activity-room shows who has joined an activity and where its time stands. Joining is not accepting a time. Drafting, planning, invitations and RSVPs are done in an assistant that shows the Complete card, or on the Complete website. Complete is for people 13 and over with a guardian, 18 and over otherwise.
